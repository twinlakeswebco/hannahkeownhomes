Community context

Start with how you want to live.

Commute, space, school boundaries, shopping, and access to Fort Knox can matter as much as the house itself. Hannah helps compare the tradeoffs without reducing a community to a generic ranking.

Elizabethtown

A regional hub with established neighborhoods, newer development, healthcare, shopping, dining, and direct interstate access.

Radcliff and Vine Grove

Communities closely connected to Fort Knox, with a mix of established homes, newer construction, and military-family demand.

Leitchfield and Grayson County

More rural surroundings, lake-area access, small-town character, and a broader mix of homes, land, and acreage.

Relocation workflow

A shorter learning curve.
