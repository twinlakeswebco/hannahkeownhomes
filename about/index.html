About Hannah

Local guidance with a higher standard of care.

Hannah Keown is a Kentucky REALTOR® with Schuler Bauer Real Estate Services ERA Powered, serving buyers and sellers throughout Central Kentucky.

Clear, direct, personal

A real estate process built around people.

Hannah helps buyers, sellers, veterans, first-time homeowners, and relocating families make informed decisions without losing sight of what the move means to them.

Her approach centers on honest strategy, responsive communication, and preparation at each stage. Sellers receive professional presentation, a property-specific marketing plan, and a complimentary 2-10 Home Buyers Warranty. VA buyers receive guidance from an agent who understands the financing process and the pace of a military move.
