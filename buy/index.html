The path to closing

Seven decisions, handled in sequence.

1

Clarify the move

Set priorities for location, timing, budget, property type, and the daily life the home needs to support.

2

Prepare financing

Compare loan options, obtain a useful preapproval, and understand cash needed for the offer and closing.

3

Search with context

Evaluate homes alongside neighborhood, condition, resale, commute, and loan-program considerations.

4

Write the offer

Balance price, timing, contingencies, seller priorities, and your protection as a buyer.

5

Inspect and appraise

Complete buyer-selected due diligence while the lender confirms value and applicable property standards.

6

Complete the loan

Respond to lender requests, confirm insurance, review title work, and protect every contract deadline.

7

Close with clarity

Review final figures, complete the walkthrough, sign, receive possession, and know what happens next.
