Privacy Policy

Hannah Keown Homes respects your privacy. Guide requests collect your email address, the guide selected, and the time and page of your request. The free market analysis form collects the contact information, property details, moving plans, and communication preferences you choose to submit.

Information you choose to share

If you submit a form, call, or text Hannah, the information you provide is used to respond to your inquiry, prepare your market analysis, and support real estate services. Please do not send sensitive financial or personal information by text.

Form processing and communication preferences

Guide and market analysis requests are processed through Web3Forms and delivered by email to the recipient associated with this website's form. Submitted details and communication preferences are included in that email. Guide requests are used to provide the requested resource and respond to your inquiry; requesting a guide does not enroll you in marketing texts or calls. See Web3Forms' Privacy Policy for its processing practices.

The form provides separate optional choices for marketing texts and calls. Those choices are not required to request an analysis. If you opt in to texts, message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. Contact Hannah at (270) 589-8376 to update your communication preferences or ask about information you have submitted.

Third-party websites

This website links to third-party services, including Pennington Properties, guide providers, lenders, and social media profiles. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Questions

For privacy questions, call or text (270) 589-8376.
